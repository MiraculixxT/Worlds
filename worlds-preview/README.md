# 🌄 Show My World

Your worlds as the title screen panorama.

Selecting a world or server displays your world in the panorama, directly where you left the last time!<br>
Tiered of the vanilla panorama? Set your last played world or a random every time as default!


![preview](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Y3VSkUwV/images/c84217c27f80d074434660836d83f704e7b2bac9.png)

## Settings

Supports shaders, mods & resource-packs out of the box!

| Setting          | What it does                                                           |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Auto Capture     | Capture on world leave                                                 |
| Default Panorama | What shows with nothing selected: *Vanilla*, *Last Played* or *Random* |
| Fade (ms)        | Cross-fade length, `0` switches instantly                              |
| Toggle           | Toggle off override for testing                                        |

Config lives in `config/showmyworld/settings.json` or via ModMenu.
Panoramas are saved in `<world>/panorama/screenshots/` in vanilla format, 
place your own panorama in `<world>/panorama/` to override auto-mode!

---

Directly integrated by [**Worlds**](https://modrinth.com/mod/world) with more useful world configurations & faster loading!
